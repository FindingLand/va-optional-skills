## 4. Appeal form

Link to the filled form in the same folder, or the town form's questions with answers if it cannot be filled. Signature and date left blank.

## 5. Filing instructions

- Informal review first, if offered: [who, how to book, by when]
- Formal filing: [board, address, email if accepted, in person or by mail], whether a postmark counts, deadline [DATE]
- Bring to a hearing: two printed copies of this packet, photos, the rent roll if income is argued
- Decision expected: [WHEN]
- If the owner disagrees with the decision: the next step and its deadline are in the policy file. Talk to Vera and the attorney before deciding.
