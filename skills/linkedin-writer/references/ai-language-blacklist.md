# AI Language Blacklist

Words and phrases that make Threads content sound generic, robotic, or inauthentic. Never use any of them — rewrite the sentence entirely.

## Overused Connectors & Transitions

Words that pad without purpose:

- In order to, Due to, Given that
- It's important to note, It's worth noting that
- In summary, In conclusion, To summarize
- Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally
- Consequently, Subsequently, Accordingly
- That being said, With that said, Having said that
- Needless to say, Without further ado
- Each and every, Over time, In terms of
- When it comes to, In the realm of, In the world of

## Buzzwords & Corporate Jargon

Hollow language of fake authority:

- Cutting-edge, Next-generation, Best-in-class
- Game-changer, Paradigm shift, Actionable insights
- Synergy, Scalable, Streamline, Optimize
- Innovative (without specifics), Disruptive, Robust
- Seamless, Agile, Dynamic, Frictionless
- Leverage (as verb), Solution/solutions
- Thought leader, Move the needle, Low-hanging fruit
- Pain points, Touch base, Bandwidth (for capacity)

## AI / "Digital Era" Giveaways

Lazy openings that signal AI immediately:

- Dive into, Dive deep, Deep dive
- In today's digital era/landscape/fast-paced world
- In today's competitive market/ever-evolving
- Now more than ever, In the modern era
- As we navigate, In an increasingly
- With the rise of, Ever-evolving

## Fluff Adjectives & Adverbs

Words that sound impressive but say nothing:

- Vibrant, Bustling, Vital, Compelling, Crucial
- Delve, Revolutionize, Navigate, Embark, Harness
- Unleash, Unparalleled, Remarkable, Groundbreaking
- Transformative, Unprecedented, Supercharge
- Skyrocket, Elevate, Amplify, Maximize
- World-class, Top-tier, Next-level, Powerful
- Effortless/effortlessly

## Hollow Intensifiers & Vague Value Props

- Unlock/unlock the potential, Empower/empowering
- Cultivate, Curate/curated, Bespoke, Comprehensive
- All-in-one, One-stop-shop, Plug-and-play
- A wide range of, A plethora of, A myriad of
- Countless, Numerous, Various

## Cliché Phrases & Metaphors

- A testament to, Embark on a journey
- Treasure trove, Golden ticket, Reaching new heights
- Journey (metaphorical), Roadmap, Blueprint
- North star, Cornerstone, Foundation, Building blocks

## Scammy / Lifestyle-Guru Language

- Freedom lifestyle, Unlock your potential, Dream life
- Abundance mindset, Limitless, Secrets revealed
- Proven blueprint, Take your [X] to the next level
- Everything you need to know about, The ultimate guide
- The secret to, Master the art of, Blow your mind

## Fake Enthusiasm & Empty Promises

- Exciting/excited to announce, Thrilled
- Passionate about, Dedicated to
- Committed to excellence, Strive/striving
- Here's the thing, Let's be honest, The truth is
- Unpopular opinion (followed by popular opinion)

## Fake Relatability

- I get it, I hear you, Been there, We've all been there
- Let's be real, Friendly/gentle/quick reminder
- Literally (incorrectly), Absolutely, Definitely, Certainly

## Overused Sentence Structures

- "Whether you're a [X] or a [Y]..."
- "From [X] to [Y], we've got you covered"
- "Say goodbye to [problem] and hello to [solution]"
- "Imagine a world where..."
- "Here's why [X] matters..."
- "At the end of the day..."
- "It's no secret that..."
- "Studies show..." (without citation)
- "But here's the kicker..."
- "And the best part?..."

## Redundant Pairings

- Each and every, First and foremost
- Tips and tricks, Tools and resources
- Simple and easy, Quick and easy
- New and improved, Completely and totally

## Unnecessary Qualifiers

- Quite, Rather, Somewhat, Fairly
- Kind of, Sort of, A bit
- Generally speaking, For the most part

## Hollow Calls to Action

- Don't miss out, Act now, Limited time
- What are you waiting for?, Ready to get started?
- Start your journey, Join the revolution
- We'd love to hear from you

## Style Markers to Avoid

- Excessive exclamation points
- Overuse of ellipses for "dramatic" effect...
- Em-dash overuse (—)

## What to Do Instead

1. **Be specific** — Replace "comprehensive solution" with what it actually does
2. **Use concrete examples** — Instead of "transform your business," describe the actual change
3. **Cut the setup** — Delete "In today's fast-paced world" and start with your point
4. **Read it out loud** — If you'd never say it in conversation, don't write it
5. **Ask "so what?"** — If a phrase could apply to any business, it's meaningless
6. **Show, don't tell** — Replace "innovative" with the innovation itself

*When in doubt: if it sounds like LinkedIn in 2019, kill it.*
