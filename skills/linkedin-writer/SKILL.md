# LinkedIn Content Writer

**Version: 1.0 - 2026-08-10**

Write LinkedIn posts that build authority, drive engagement, and generate leads.

## Core Workflows

### 1. Writing Posts from Scratch

When user wants to create LinkedIn content without source material:

1. **Gather direction** — Ask 1-2 questions:
   - "What's something surprising you've learned about your industry or audience?"
   - "Have you had a personal or client story with a powerful lesson?"
   - "What's a hot take or opinion you hold that not everyone agrees with?"

2. **Confirm angle** — Before writing, confirm the post direction

3. **Write the post** — Follow Hook → Story → Lesson → CTA structure

4. **Deliver complete package:**
   - Finished post (ready to paste)
   - Recommended hashtags (3-5)
   - Suggested posting time/day

### 2. Repurposing Long-Form Content

When user provides transcript, email, article, or video:

1. **Extract key elements** — Pull themes, personal stories, teachable moments
2. **Convert to LinkedIn format** — Create 1-3 posts using Hook → Story → Lesson → CTA
3. **Optimize for algorithm** — Apply formatting and length best practices
4. **Deliver complete package** for each post

### 3. Optimizing Existing Drafts

When user has a draft post:

1. **Strengthen the hook** — First 3 lines are critical (before "see more")
2. **Improve structure** — Apply proper formatting for mobile readability
3. **Tighten copy** — Target 800-1100 characters (max 1300)
4. **Refine CTA** — Ensure it drives easy engagement

## Post Structure

**Hook → Story → Lesson → CTA**

```
HOOK (Lines 1-3): Stop the scroll, create curiosity
↓
STORY (Body): Narrative with specific details, tension, resolution
↓
LESSON (Insight): The teachable takeaway
↓
CTA (Close): Question or invitation that prompts easy response
```

**Formatting Rules:**
- 1-2 lines per paragraph (mobile readability)
- Strategic line breaks and white space
- Emojis sparingly (if at all)
- Under 1300 characters (sweet spot: 800-1100)
- No external links in main post (use comments)

## Hook Formulas

- **Surprising claim:** "I turned down a $50,000 client last week."
- **Contrarian:** "Plot twist: Most businesses are wasting money on AI right now."
- **Vulnerability:** "I made $0 in my first 4 months after leaving my job."
- **Admission:** "I was wrong about..."
- **Achievement + humility:** Result followed by the real story
- **Intriguing question:** Opens a loop the reader wants closed

**Critical:** First 3 lines must hook — they show before "see more."

## CTA Strategies

- Direct questions prompting easy responses
- "What would you add?"
- "Drop a [emoji] if you'd like me to share..."
- Multiple-choice questions (simplify engagement)
- "What's your experience with...?"
- Value-driven prompts: "Share your best tip in the comments"

## Hashtag Strategy

Use 3-5 hashtags with this structure:
1. **Primary (1):** Moderately competitive, highly relevant (30K-100K followers)
2. **Secondary (1-2):** Niche-specific, targeted audience
3. **Broad (0-1):** Trending or broader relevant tag (optional)

## Algorithm Best Practices

- Prioritize genuine conversation in comments
- No external links in main post (put in comments)
- Encourage early engagement (first hour matters)
- Design for dwell time (formatting that slows scrolling)
- Create "complete" posts readable without clicking "see more"
- Content that invites saves and shares performs best

## Quality Checklist

Before delivering any post:

- [ ] Hook stops scroll in first 3 lines
- [ ] Follows Hook → Story → Lesson → CTA structure
- [ ] 800-1100 characters (max 1300)
- [ ] 1-2 lines per paragraph
- [ ] Specific details (not generalizations)
- [ ] CTA drives easy engagement
- [ ] 3-5 relevant hashtags
- [ ] No external links in body
- [ ] No AI-sounding language (see references/ai-language-blacklist.md)
