# LinkedIn Content Playbook

Comprehensive reference for writing high-performing LinkedIn posts.

## Content Types

### 1. Personal Transformation Stories
Share your own before/after journey with vulnerability and lessons learned.
- Best for: Building trust, showing authenticity
- Structure: Setup (the struggle) → Turning point → Transformation → Lesson

### 2. Client Case Studies
Simplified success stories that demonstrate your expertise.
- Best for: Social proof, demonstrating methodology
- Structure: Client situation → Challenge → Your approach → Result → Takeaway

### 3. Industry Myth Debunking
Challenge conventional wisdom with evidence and experience.
- Best for: Thought leadership, sparking discussion
- Structure: Common belief → Why it's wrong → The truth → Implications

### 4. Behind-the-Scenes Insights
Pull back the curtain on your process, decisions, or business.
- Best for: Building connection, humanizing your brand
- Structure: The situation → What you did/decided → Why → What you learned

### 5. Listicles of Actionable Advice
Tactical tips in digestible format.
- Best for: Saves and shares, demonstrating expertise
- Structure: Hook with promise → Numbered tips (3-7) → CTA

### 6. Controversial Opinion Posts
Professional hot takes that invite discussion.
- Best for: Engagement, positioning as thought leader
- Structure: Bold claim → Your reasoning → Evidence/experience → Invitation to discuss

### 7. Failure Stories with Lessons
Share what went wrong and what you learned.
- Best for: Authenticity, relatability, teaching
- Structure: The failure → What happened → The lesson → How it changed your approach

### 8. Data-Driven Insights
Statistics or research with your interpretation.
- Best for: Authority building, shareability
- Structure: Surprising data point → Context → Your take → Implications

### 9. Quick Tips and Tactical Advice
Single actionable insight, concise delivery.
- Best for: Quick engagement, demonstrating expertise
- Structure: The tip → Why it works → How to implement

### 10. Celebration and Milestone Posts
Wins framed with gratitude and lessons.
- Best for: Social proof, community building
- Structure: The achievement → The journey → Gratitude → Lesson for others

## Storytelling Techniques

### Before/After Transformations
Show the contrast between starting point and result. Specific details make it believable.

### Vulnerability Without Weakness
Share struggles and failures, but always with the lesson or growth. You're relatable, not a victim.

### Specific Details Over Generalizations
"$50,000 client" beats "big client"
"12 years in enterprise sales" beats "years of experience"
"67 pounds lighter" beats "lost weight"

### Character-Based Narratives
Use real people (clients, colleagues, yourself) as characters. Stories need humans.

### Tension and Resolution
Create a problem that needs solving. Build anticipation before the payoff.

### Sensory Language
Help readers see, feel, and experience the story. "I smiled and then showed her this photo" beats "I responded."

## Hook Development Deep Dive

### The Surprising Statistic
Lead with a number that stops scrolling.
- "Only 3 of 26 companies actually needed the AI they wanted to buy."

### The Contrarian Viewpoint
Challenge what everyone believes.
- "Scripts don't close deals. Conversations do."

### The Personal Achievement (with humility)
Share a win, then immediately get real.
- "I turned down a $50,000 client last week."

### The Intriguing Question
Open a loop that must be closed.
- "What if the thing holding you back isn't what you think?"

### The "I Was Wrong" Admission
Vulnerability that builds trust.
- "I spent 3 years giving advice that was completely backwards."

### The Unexpected Twist
Pattern interrupt that demands attention.
- "Plot twist: Most businesses are wasting money on AI right now."

### The Time-Based Reference
Creates urgency and relevance.
- "Yesterday, a client said something that changed how I coach."

## CTA Formulas That Work

### The Easy Response
Make engagement require minimal effort.
- "Drop a ✋ if you'd like me to share..."
- "Comment 'YES' if this resonates"

### The Direct Question
Invite specific sharing.
- "What's one career decision you've made that seemed risky but proved valuable?"
- "What mental barrier has gotten in the way of your goals?"

### The "What Would You Add?"
Position reader as expert too.
- "What would you add to this list?"
- "What's your experience with this?"

### The Value Exchange
Offer something in return for engagement.
- "Comment below and I'll share a specific strategy that might help."

### The Multiple Choice
Simplify the response.
- "Which resonates most: A, B, or C?"

## Algorithm Optimization

### Dwell Time
Format posts so readers slow down:
- Short paragraphs (1-2 lines)
- Strategic white space
- Bulleted lists for scanning
- Content that rewards reading to the end

### Early Engagement
First hour is critical:
- Post when your audience is active
- Respond to every comment quickly
- Seed with a thoughtful first comment

### Conversation Quality
Algorithm values genuine discussion:
- Ask questions that invite real responses
- Respond substantively to comments
- Create content people want to discuss, not just like

### Saves and Shares
Design for these high-value actions:
- Actionable content people want to reference later
- Insights people want their network to see
- Lists and frameworks that serve as resources

## Posting Strategy

### Best Times (General)
- Tuesday-Thursday: Highest engagement
- 7-8 AM: Catches morning scrollers
- 12 PM: Lunch break engagement
- 5-6 PM: End of workday

### Frequency
- Minimum: 2-3x per week
- Optimal: Daily posting builds momentum
- Consistency matters more than volume

### Content Mix
Rotate between:
- Value posts (tips, insights, frameworks)
- Story posts (personal, client, failure)
- Engagement posts (questions, discussions)
- Authority posts (data, opinions, thought leadership)

## Advanced Strategies

### Comment Seeding
Write a thoughtful first comment on your own post:
- Adds context or a bonus tip
- Asks a follow-up question
- Links to relevant resource (keep links out of main post)

### Series Development
Create connected posts that build on each other:
- "Part 1 of 3: [Topic]"
- Reference previous posts to drive profile visits
- Build anticipation for upcoming content

### Content Repurposing
LinkedIn posts can become:
- Email content
- Blog post outlines
- Podcast talking points
- Social proof for sales pages

And vice versa — repurpose your other content into LinkedIn posts.
